Our Story, Our Passion, Our Soul

Our Story

Whip My Soul is a family-owned soul food restaurant proudly serving the Austin, Texas community. Run by a dedicated husband and wife team, our restaurant was born from a deep love of traditional southern cooking and the desire to share those flavors with everyone.

Every recipe we serve is inspired by generations of southern cooking traditions passed down through our family. From our famous catfish fillets and fried chicken to our slow-braised oxtails, each dish is made from scratch with the freshest ingredients and the most important ingredient of all — love.

We believe that food has the power to bring people together, to comfort the soul, and to create lasting memories. That's why everything on our menu is homemade, from our Sweet Glazed Yams and Mean Greens to our legendary Cheezy Mac and Red Beans and Rice.
